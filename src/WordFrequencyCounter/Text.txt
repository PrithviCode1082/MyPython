Once upon a time, in a house filled with soft blankets and sunny spots, lived a tiny kitten named Mochi. Mochi was only four weeks old, but Mochi believed he was a mighty tiger. Being a tiny kitten is a very busy job! Every morning, Mochi would wake up and stretch his tiny paws. His tiny paws were pink and soft, like little beans. Mochi loved his tiny paws, especially when Mochi used those tiny paws to bat at a floating speck of dust in the sunlight.
The world was a very big place for such a tiny kitten. Mochi lived in a cozy living room with a giant sofa. To Mochi, the sofa was a massive mountain. Mochi would wiggle his tiny tail and prepare to climb the mountain. Mochi would jump! Mochi would tumble! Mochi would try again because Mochi was a brave kitten. After Mochi finished climbing the mountain, Mochi would find a blue yarn ball. The yarn ball was Mochi’s favorite toy. Mochi would chase the yarn ball under the chair, and then Mochi would chase the yarn ball back to the rug.
When Mochi got tired from all the chasing and climbing, Mochi would look for a snack. A four-week-old kitten needs a lot of energy to grow into a big tiger! Mochi would meow a tiny meow until he got his bowl of milk. After Mochi finished his milk, Mochi had a very messy face. Mochi would try to wash his face with his tiny paws, but Mochi usually just ended up falling over.
In the afternoon, the sun would hit the rug, creating a warm golden circle. Mochi loved the warm sun. Mochi would curl up into a small, round ball—just like a real mochi dessert—and start to purr. The purr was a tiny sound, like a little motor running inside a kitten. Mochi would dream about giant yarn balls and mountains made of blankets. While Mochi slept, his tiny paws would twitch as if Mochi were still chasing butterflies in his dreams.
Being a kitten named Mochi was a wonderful life. Every day brought a new adventure for Mochi and his tiny paws. Whether Mochi was attacking a shoelace or napping in the sun, Mochi was the happiest kitten in the whole world. Everyone who met Mochi would smile because Mochi was just so small and so sweet. Mochi was more than just a kitten; Mochi was a tiny hero in a big, soft world.
